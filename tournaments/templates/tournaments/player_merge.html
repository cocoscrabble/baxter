Merge Guest Players

These guest players have the same name as a player with a CoCo number. Usually that is one person, entered as a guest before their number came through. Merging moves the guest's tournament entries and results onto the CoCo player and deletes the guest. It is recorded in the activity log of every tournament the guest played in, and cannot be undone from here.

The match is by name only, so check it is the same person first. A merge is refused if both players are entered in the same division.
