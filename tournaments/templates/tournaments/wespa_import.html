WESPA ratings

Baxter keeps its own copy of the WESPA rating list. A WESPA rating is used only for a player who has no CoCo rating, and it is never sent to the central database — it is Baxter's copy of somebody else's number.

The list is held in full, including the thousands of players Baxter has never seen, because those are the ones it is for: an overseas visitor with no CoCo number can be found by name at the registration desk instead of having their rating typed in from a website. Pulling the list does not add anyone to the roster — a WESPA player becomes a Baxter player when you enter them.

This does not disturb any tournament in progress: entrants pin the rating they were seeded from when they enter, so a refresh cannot reshuffle pairings.
